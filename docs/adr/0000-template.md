# NNNN. FILL: the decision, as a short noun phrase

- **Status**: Proposed
  <!-- Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN -->
- **Date**: FILL (ISO 8601)

## Context

FILL: the forces at play, stated neutrally. What was true, what was constrained,
what was unknown. Write it so somebody who disagrees with the decision would
still accept this section as fair.

## Decision

FILL: what was decided, in the active voice and the past tense. Name the
alternative that was rejected and why. One decision per record.

## Consequences

FILL: what becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what is now expensive to
reverse. The costs are the point: a record that lists only benefits is
advocacy, and advocacy is not a decision record.
