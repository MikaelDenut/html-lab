Climb Mount Pebblewhistle

This page is a mountain trail. Scroll down slowly: each yellow sign stops at the top of the window and waits, and the next sign stacks under it, like the arrows on a signpost.

The trick is position: sticky. Find the block called STICKY SIGNS in style.css.

Village 1200 m

The trail starts at the fountain in the village square. Fill your water bottle here: there is no shop until the hut at the top.

Pine forest 1500 m

The path climbs in zigzags under tall pine trees. It is cool and shady, and it smells of resin. Count the pine cones on the path.

Flower meadow 1900 m

Above the trees, the meadow is full of tiny flowers. Listen for a sharp whistle: marmots whistle to warn their family when someone is coming.

Rocks and snow 2400 m

Now the path is made of rocks, and some snow stays all summer. Put on a jumper: on average the air gets about 6.5 degrees colder for every 1000 metres you climb.

Kettlecap Hut 2810 m

You made it! The hut has hot soup, bunk beds and a view over three valleys. Now scroll back up and watch the signs let go, one by one.
