Far things move slowly

Look out of a car window. The fence by the road zooms past. The trees further away go slower. The mountains far away hardly move at all. This is called parallax.

A mountain in five layers

Scroll inside this window. The trees move at full speed, the hills slower, the far peaks slower still, the sun slowest of all, and the sky not at all.

Scroll down inside me

  • Sky background does not move
  • Sun translateZ(-3px) scale(4) one quarter speed
  • Far peaks translateZ(-2px) scale(3) one third speed
  • Near hills translateZ(-1px) scale(2) half speed
  • Front trees transform: none full speed

translateZ pushes a layer away from you, deeper into the screen. Far away things look smaller, so scale makes the layer big again. Now it looks the same, but it scrolls slower. The window needs perspective: 1px to make the screen deep.

A hole in the page

Now scroll the whole page and watch the band below. The photo stays still and the band slides over it.

The band is a hole in the page. The cloud photo behind it is pinned to the window with background-attachment: fixed.

If the layers do not move

Some people feel sick when things on the screen slide at different speeds. They can ask their computer for less motion. Then this page shows the mountain flat, and the photo scrolls with the band. The CSS checks with prefers-reduced-motion.
