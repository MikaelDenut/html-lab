How a train stays on the rails

On most railways in the world the two rails are 1435 mm apart. That is called standard gauge. In Finland the rails are a little wider: 1524 mm.

Every train wheel has a lip on its inside edge, called a flange. The flanges hug the rails, so the wheels stay on them, even round a bend.
