How to travel with a bike

  1. Buy a bike ticket, and one for yourself.
  2. Wait at the bike sign at the end of the platform. The bike carriage stops right there.
  3. Let people off first. Then walk your bike on. Never ride on the platform.
  4. Strap your bike in the bike space and stay close to it.
