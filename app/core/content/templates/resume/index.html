I draw friendly pictures for children's books.

Foxes, whales, lighthouses and very small dragons. I turn stories into pictures that make readers smile, from the first sketch to the printed page.

About me

I grew up by the sea and filled every notebook with boats and birds. Today I work in a small studio above a bakery, so it always smells of bread.

I like to start with a pencil and paper. Then I paint with watercolours or on my drawing tablet. A good picture tells a part of the story that the words do not say.
