Safety first

  • Wear safety glasses on the factory floor.
  • Wear ear protectors near the tube cutter.
  • Walk on the green paths. Forklifts drive on the grey ones.
  • Tie long hair back near machines that spin.
  • See something wrong? Press the big red stop button and tell a team leader.
