Our mission story

Comet Kite is a pretend mission, made up for this website. The planets are real, and so are the facts!

First our ship swings around the Moon. Then it zooms to Mars, the red planet. After that comes the long ride to giant Jupiter, and to Saturn with its shiny rings.

Real spaceships need years to fly that far. So the crew packs books, games and lots of space food.

Four worlds, three friends and one robot. Meet the whole crew.

Launch day, step by step

The mission clock counts hours, minutes and seconds. T-00:10:00 means 10 minutes before lift-off. T+00:02:00 means 2 minutes after lift-off.

  1. T-03:00:00

    Climb aboard

    The crew puts on spacesuits, climbs in and buckles up.

  2. T-00:10:00

    Final checks

    Mission control checks every part of the ship. Everyone says "Go!"

  3. T-00:00:10

    Countdown

    Ten, nine, eight, seven, six, five, four, three, two, one...

  4. T-00:00:00

    Lift-off!

    The engines roar and the rocket climbs into the sky.

  5. T+00:02:00

    Boosters drop away

    The empty side rockets fall off. The ship gets lighter and faster.

  6. T+00:09:00

    Hello, space!

    The engines stop. Comet Kite is in orbit, flying round and round the Earth, and the crew starts to float.
