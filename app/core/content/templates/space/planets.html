Worlds on our trip

Comet Kite starts on Earth and visits four more worlds. Put your mouse on a card and watch it light up in its own colour!

Earth

Planet 3 from the Sun

Our home, where the trip begins. It is the only planet where we know there is life.

  • 1 moon
  • Day: 24 hours

The Moon

Earth's moon

There is no air and no wind here, so footprints stay for a very long time.

  • About 384,000 km away
  • Gravity: 1/6 of Earth's

Mars

Planet 4 from the Sun

Mars is red because its dust is rusty. It has the tallest volcano in the Solar System.

  • 2 moons
  • Day: a bit over 24 hours

Jupiter

Planet 5 from the Sun

The biggest planet of all. More than 1,300 Earths could fit inside it.

  • Day: about 10 hours
  • A giant ball of gas

Saturn

Planet 6 from the Sun

Its rings are made of ice and rock. Saturn is so light that it would float in a giant bathtub.

  • Day: about 10.5 hours
  • Year: about 29 Earth years
