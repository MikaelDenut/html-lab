How the crew trained

Real astronauts train for years before they fly. Our crew did the same things.

  • The giant pool

    They practised space walks under water, in a huge pool. Floating in water feels a bit like floating in space.

  • The floating plane

    A special plane flies up and down like a roller coaster. At the top of each hill, everyone floats for about 20 seconds.

  • Survival camp

    They learned to keep warm, build a shelter and call for help, in case the capsule lands far from home.

  • The gym, every day

    In space, muscles and bones get weaker. So astronauts exercise for about two hours every day.
