Why stars twinkle

Stars twinkle because the air around Earth is always moving. Up in space, above the air, stars do not twinkle at all.

Some people feel dizzy when things move. If their Mac asks for less motion, the stars stand still. See the last block in style.css. Help: Less motion for some people
