Questions

Can I take photos?

Yes, but no flash, please. A flash makes a bright spot on the glass and bothers other visitors.

Can I touch the paintings?

Please do not. Our skin leaves a little oil and dirt behind, and over many years that harms the paint.

Why is one room so dark?

Strong light makes watercolours and paper fade. That is why the real Young Hare by Dürer is only shown now and then.

Is there a lift?

Yes, next to the way in. It fits a wheelchair or a pram.

Can I bring my snail?

Yes, if it stays in its box. Snails get in free.
