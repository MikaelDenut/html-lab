Grid layouts, up close

A grid is a net of lines, and you put boxes between the lines. Every grid on this page has hidden lines. Flip the switch to see them: each line runs along the gap between two dashed boxes.

Twelve columns

Many studios start a page with twelve thin columns. Twelve splits into halves, thirds, quarters and sixths. The numbers on top sit on the lines.

grid-template-columns: repeat(12, 1fr);
span 12 span 8 span 4 span 4 span 4 span 4 span 12

Boxes between lines

Four columns and three rows make twelve spaces. The big box starts at line 1 and stops at line 3, across and down. Count the lines with the switch on.

grid-column: 1 / 3;
grid-row: 1 / 3;
1 / 3

Areas with names

Draw a little map of the page with words. Each word names an area. Write a word twice and its box gets wider.

grid-template-areas:
  "head head head"
  "menu main side"
  "foot foot foot";
head menu main side foot

Fixed and stretchy

Mix two kinds of size. The first column is always 120 pixels wide. The second one, 1fr, takes what is left, so it grows when the window grows.

grid-template-columns: 120px 1fr;
120px 1fr 1fr
