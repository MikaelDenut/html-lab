Line things up with flexbox

Flexbox puts boxes in a row and decides where the empty space goes. Change one word and the boxes move.

Start

123
justify-content: flex-start;

The boxes line up on the left. Flexbox does this if you say nothing. Good for a row of tags.

Center

123
justify-content: center;

The boxes huddle in the middle, with the same space on both sides. Good for a title on a banner. CSS spells it the American way: center.

Space between

123
justify-content: space-between;

The first box goes left, the last box goes right. Look at the bar at the top of this page: logo on the left, menu on the right.

Wrap

1234 567
flex-wrap: wrap;

No room left? The boxes jump to the next row instead of getting squashed.
