After-school clubs

Seven clubs, five days, zero homework. Every club is free, and you can join at any time.

Garden club

Mondays, 15.15 to 16.15, in the school garden

Grow vegetables, flowers and, yes, giant pumpkins. This term we plant bulbs for spring and cook Big Bert soup.

See how to join

Ask Mr Ashdown in class 5. Bring rain boots or old shoes.

Chess club

Tuesdays, 15.15, library

Learn the moves, solve puzzles and play friendly games. Beginners are very welcome.

Robot club

Wednesdays, 15.15, computer room

Build small robots and tell them what to do with code. This term: a robot that draws circles.

Choir

Thursdays, lunchtime, music room

Sing songs from all over the world. In December we sing at the winter concert.

Comic club

Thursdays, 15.15, art room

Draw your own comics, from one funny picture to a whole story with heroes.

Running club

Fridays, 15.15, school field

Short, fun runs and running games. Every runner gets a stamp card.

Newspaper team

Wednesdays, 15.15, library

Find stories, ask questions and write for the Post. Good at noticing things? Come along.
