Giant pumpkin wins the school garden show

Big Bert grew from one small seed. Now he is too heavy for one pupil to lift.

On Monday the garden club carried a pumpkin into the school hall. It took four pupils and one wheelbarrow. The pumpkin is called Big Bert, and he weighs 38 kilograms.

"We planted the seed in May," says Noor from class 5. "We gave it water every single day, even in the holidays."

Big Bert won first prize at the garden show. The judges said he was the roundest and the most orange.
