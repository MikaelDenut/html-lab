Meet the heroes

On a computer, move your mouse over a card and watch it tilt!

Captain Puddlejump

The hero

Jumps into one puddle and pops out of another one, anywhere in Puddle City.

  • Puddle jumping
  • Kindness
  • Staying dry

Mizzle

The rain cloud

Makes a puddle in one second. Mizzle is an English word for very light rain.

  • Making rain
  • Floating
  • Hiding (always drips)

Granny Gumboots

The inventor

Builds clever boots in her shed. Gumboots is another word for rain boots.

  • Inventing
  • Baking
  • Running

Baron Bone-Dry

Grumpy, but getting better

He tried to drink every puddle in town. Now he jumps in them with his new boots!

  • Grumpiness
  • Sock collecting
  • Puddle jumping
