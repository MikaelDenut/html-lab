The Great Puddle Grab

Issue 1. Read the panels like a book: left to right, then down.

Puddle City. It rained all day long.

Hooray! Puddles everywhere!

DRIP! DROP!

Meanwhile, on a roof...

Wet socks AGAIN? My Super Sponge will drink every puddle in town!

SLUUURP!

The next morning...

Where did all the puddles go? No puddle, no jump!

Leave it to me! Drip, drip, drip...

SPLASH!

SPLOOSH!

In he jumps... and out he pops, on the Baron's roof!

Hello! Why do you take all the puddles?

Rain makes my socks wet. I hate wet socks!

Then try my super boots! Dry socks, every time!

Now the Baron loves puddles!

WHEEE!

The end

Sound words

SPLASH and SLURP sound like the noise they name. Words like that are called onomatopoeia. How many sound words can you find on this page?
