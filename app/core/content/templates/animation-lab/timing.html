The timing race

Every ball runs the same road in the same 2 seconds. Only the timing word is different. Put your mouse on a lane to start that ball. No mouse? Press Tab to reach a lane.

The little drawing is the timing curve: it goes up slowly where the ball is slow, and steeply where the ball is fast.

Start all! Put your mouse here and every ball runs at once.

How the race works

.runner {
  transition: transform 2s;
  transition-timing-function: ease-in;
}

.lane:hover .runner {
  transform: translateX(100%);
}

These balls use transition, not @keyframes. A transition plays when something changes: here, when your mouse comes onto the lane.

Take your mouse away and the ball runs home, with the same timing.

Try it: change 2s to 5s in style.css and the race gets slow.

Some people feel dizzy when things move. If their Mac asks for less motion, this lab stops every test. See the last block in style.css. Help: Less motion for some people
