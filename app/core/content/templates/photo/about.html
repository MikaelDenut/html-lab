I take photos of nature: animals, plants, mountains and the sky. I started when I was nine, with an old camera from my grandma.

What is in my bag

  • A camera with a long lens, to see shy animals from far away
  • A tripod with three legs, to hold the camera still
  • A rain cover, because forests are wet
  • Gloves with open fingertips, for pressing buttons in the cold
  • A notebook, to write down what I saw
  • Snacks. Waiting makes you hungry!

How I take a photo

  1. Be quiet and wait

    Animals hide when they hear you. Sit still and let them come.

  2. Look at the light

    Soft morning light makes colours glow. Midday sun makes hard shadows.

  3. Get down low

    Hold the camera at the animal's eye level, not above it.

  4. Keep the horizon straight

    A tilted sea looks like it is spilling out of the photo.

  5. Take many, keep one

    Take lots of photos. At home, pick the very best one.
