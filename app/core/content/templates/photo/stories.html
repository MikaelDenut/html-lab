Two trips, told with pictures

A story can be told with photos. Big ones set the scene, small ones show the details.

Winter and summer in the far north

Foxes have amazing ears. A fox can hear a mouse moving under the snow. Then it jumps up high and dives into the snow, nose first!

At night the sky turned green. In summer I came back to see the puffins.

A rainy day in the forest

Rain is my favourite weather for forest photos. The colours get deep and the leaves shine.

I put my camera on a tripod, so it stays very still. Then the camera can take a slow photo. The rocks stay sharp, and the moving water turns soft and white.

"Wet socks are the price of good photos."
