Our plants

A seed packet tells you how much sun and water a plant wants. Our plant cards look like seed packets, so you can read them the same way.

Full sun
Sun for most of the day.
Water often
Keep the soil damp. One drop means: water only when it has not rained for a long time.

Peas

Peas like cool weather, so they can go in early. They climb with tiny curly tendrils.

Full sun

Water when dry

Potatoes

Plant one small potato and many new ones grow under the ground. Never eat the leaves or green potatoes.

Full sun

Water when dry

Strawberries

The plants send out long runners that grow into new baby plants. The seeds are on the outside!

Full sun

Water when dry

Lettuce

Lettuce is happy in a little shade. In hot weather it grows tall and flowers, and then the leaves taste bitter.

Sun or a little shade

Water often

Sunflowers

Young sunflowers turn to follow the sun across the sky. When they are older, the flowers face east.

Full sun

Water when dry

Thyme

Thyme is a herb that likes dry, sunny places. Bees love its tiny flowers. Rub a leaf and smell it!

Full sun

Water a little
