Come and visit

Anyone can come in, read, and sit in the big window seat. To take books home, you need a library card. It is free.

Find us

1 Hosereel Square
Thimbleford

Look for the two big red doors with LIBRARY written above them. Park your bike where the fire engine used to stand.

The ramp by the left door leads straight in, and the whole library is on one floor.

Get your own library card

  1. Bring a grown-up. They sign the form with you.
  2. Fill in the form at the desk. Just your name and where you live.
  3. Get your card straight away. Write your name on the back.
  4. Choose your first books. Up to eight, for three weeks.
