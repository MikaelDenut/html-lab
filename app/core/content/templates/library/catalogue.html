How to read a shelf mark

C4

The letter is the shelf. The number counts books from the left. So C4 is the fourth book on shelf C, Magic. Every book wears its mark on a little label at the bottom of its spine.

Borrowing questions

How long can I keep a book?

Three weeks. If nobody is waiting for it, we can give you three more.

How many books can I take home?

Up to eight at once, with your own library card.

The book I want is borrowed. What now?

Ask at the desk. When it comes back, we keep it for you for one week.
