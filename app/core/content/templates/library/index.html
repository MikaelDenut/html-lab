Why do the titles go sideways?

On most books printed in English, the title on the spine runs from top to bottom. Lay the book flat with its front cover up, and you can read it like a line on a page.

On our shelves one line of CSS turns each title: writing-mode: vertical-rl;

A fire station full of books

Fire engines once rolled out through our two big red doors. Now books roll out instead, in bags, in baskets and under arms. The old fire bell still hangs above the desk.
