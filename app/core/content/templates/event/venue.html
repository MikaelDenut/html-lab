Venue

Copperwick Hall, 2 Lantern Quay. Everything is on one floor, with no steps. Find your way with the map.

Map

  1. Big Hall: shows and the paper plane contest
  2. Kitchen Lab: slime, volcanoes and ice cream
  3. Star Dome: trips into space
  4. Robot Workshop: build and race robots
  5. Cafe: sandwiches, fruit and juice
  6. Toilets and a baby changing room
  7. Garden: picnic tables and bike racks

Getting there

  • By train

    Glimmerford station is a 10 minute walk. Follow the blue footprints painted on the path.

  • By bus

    Bus 7 stops at the door. The stop is called Copperwick Hall.

  • By bike

    There are racks for 200 bikes in the garden, next to the picnic tables.

  • By car

    Parking costs 3 euros for the day. It fills up fast, so come early.

Questions and answers

Do we need to book?

No. Just come in and pay at the door. Shows start on time, so check the schedule.

Can we bring our own food?

Yes! Eat in the garden at the picnic tables. The cafe also sells sandwiches, fruit and juice.

Can we come with a buggy or a wheelchair?

Yes. All rooms are on one floor with wide doors and no steps.

What if it rains?

Then the picnic moves inside, to the back of the Big Hall. Science works in any weather.

Are the experiments safe?

Yes. Helpers in green T-shirts are in every room, and you get safety glasses in the Kitchen Lab.
