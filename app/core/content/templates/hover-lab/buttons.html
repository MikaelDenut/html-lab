Ten fun buttons

These buttons are links made with the <a> tag, because each one takes you to a card. A link takes you somewhere. A <button> does something on the page, and for that it needs JavaScript or a form.

No mouse? Hold Option and press Tab to jump from link to link.

Click a button and its card lights up. The link jumps to the card's id, and :target colours that card.

Every button starts as class="btn". A second class adds the trick.
