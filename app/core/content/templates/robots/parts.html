The parts box

Every part in our club box has a code on its drawer. Find the code, open the drawer, take what you need, and put back what you don't use.

PartCodeWhat it doesIn the box
DC motor M-03 Spins round when electricity flows through it. Swap its two wires and it spins the other way. 12
Battery holder BH-2AA Holds 2 AA batteries in a row: 1.5 volts and 1.5 volts make 3 volts. 10
AA battery AA-15 Gives 1.5 volts. The flat end goes against the spring. 40
LED LED-R A tiny light. The longer leg goes to plus (+). Always use it with a resistor. 25
Resistor R-220 Lets less electricity through, so an LED does not burn out. 25
Switch SW-1 Turns the robot on and off. Off makes a gap in the path for the electricity. 10
Wires W-RB Red for plus (+), black for minus (-). 50
Servo motor SV-9 Turns to an exact angle and stays there. Good for robot arms and heads. 6
Wheel WH-40 40 millimetres across. It pushes onto a motor shaft. 16
Googly eyes EYE-2 Do nothing at all. Every robot needs them. 60

Safety with tools

  • Use the hot glue gun only with a grown-up next to you. The tip and the glue get very hot.
  • Wear safety glasses when you cut wire. Little bits can jump up.
  • Never join plus (+) and minus (-) with just a wire. The wire and the battery get hot.
  • Keep button batteries away from small children. Swallowing one is very dangerous.
  • Take the batteries out before you change any wires.
