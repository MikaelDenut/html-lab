Your first Scribble Bot

A cup on three felt-tip pens, with a motor that shakes it. It wobbles across the paper and draws as it goes. Here are the first 3 of its 8 steps. Later you can build a Line Follower car or a Hello Hand that waves.

You need

  • 1 plastic cup
  • 3 felt-tip pens
  • 1 small motor with wires
  • 1 battery holder and 2 AA batteries
  • 1 small piece of cork
  • Strong tape and 2 googly eyes

Get your parts

Put all the parts on the table.

Give the cup legs

Tape three pens round the upside-down cup.

Make a wobble weight

Push the cork onto the motor shaft, near its edge.

Then: put the motor on top, add the batteries, connect the wires, give it a face and let it draw!
