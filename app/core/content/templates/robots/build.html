Build a Scribble Bot

A shaking cup on felt-tip pen legs that draws all by itself. It takes about 45 minutes. You need a grown-up for step 6.

You need

Not sure what a part is? Look in the parts box

Get your parts

Put all the parts on the table. Tick each one off in the list when you find it.

Give the cup legs

Turn the cup upside down. Tape three pens round the edge, tips down, caps on. Now the cup stands on its pens.

Make a wobble weight

Push the cork onto the motor shaft, near its edge and not in the middle. Now one side is heavier.

Why does it wobble?

When the motor spins, the heavy side of the cork pulls this way, then that way, very fast. That shakes the whole robot.

Some phones buzz the same way: inside is a tiny motor with a weight that is not in the middle.

Put the motor on top

Tape the motor flat on top of the cup. The cork sticks out past the edge, so it can spin round freely.

Add the battery holder

Tape the battery holder to the side of the cup. Keep the batteries out for now.

Connect the wires

Join the red wire of the motor to the red wire of the holder. Then join black to black. Twist the ends together and tape them.

Ask a grown-up to help with this step.

Give it a face

Stick on the googly eyes. Now your robot can see! (No, it can't. But it looks like it can.)

Let it draw

Take the caps off the pens. Put the batteries in and set your robot on a big sheet of paper. Watch it shake and scribble!

Does it only spin on the spot? Move the cork a little, or bend one pen leg out.
