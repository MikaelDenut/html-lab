Often asked

Is Homeworm free?

Yes. The Seedling plan is free for one child, for as long as you like. Bigger plans cost a little each month.

What age is it for?

Kids from about 7 to 12 years old. The buttons are big and the words are short.

Can grandma see the plan too?

Yes, on the Family plan. Up to four grown-ups can see the plan on their own phones.

What can I get for my stars?

Your family picks the treats together. A walk to the park, a film night or pancakes on Sunday.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes. Your list is saved on the phone. The family view updates when you are online again.

How do I stop my plan?

Open Settings, then Plan, then Stop. Your lists stay safe on the free plan.
