Questions

What if I am not tall enough for a ride?

At the gate we measure you and give you a coloured wristband. The colour shows which rides you can go on.

Can we bring a picnic?

Yes! There are picnic tables in Monkey Woods, in the shade of the trees.

What happens when it rains?

Most rides keep going in the rain. Soggy Sock Falls makes you wet anyway!

What if I lose my grown-up?

Find a park helper in a purple T-shirt. They take you to the Main Gate, and we call your grown-up.
