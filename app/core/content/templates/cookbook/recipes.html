Raspberry chia jars

Banana pancakes

  • 20 min
  • 8 small ones

Only two things: one ripe banana and two eggs. Mash, mix and fry little circles.

Ask an adult: hot pan

What you need
  • 1 ripe banana
  • 2 eggs
  • A little butter for the pan

Lunch For hungry lunchtimes

Rainbow wraps

  • 15 min
  • Serves 2

Spread cream cheese on a wrap, add carrot, cucumber and lettuce, and roll it up tight.

Ask an adult: knife and grater

What you need
  • 2 soft wraps
  • 2 tablespoons cream cheese
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • Half a cucumber, cut in sticks
  • A few lettuce leaves

Cheesy pea pasta

  • 20 min
  • Serves 3

Pasta and peas cook in the same pot. Stir in butter and cheese and it is ready.

Ask an adult: boiling water

What you need
  • 250 g small pasta
  • 2 cups frozen peas
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 cup grated cheese

Snacks For in between

  • Frozen banana pops

    • 10 min and 3 hours
    • 4 pops

    Dip half-bananas in yogurt, roll them in crushed cornflakes, then freeze.

    Ask an adult: cut the bananas

    What you need
    • 2 bananas, cut in half
    • 4 ice lolly sticks
    • 4 tablespoons yogurt
    • A handful of cornflakes

  • Popcorn

    • 10 min
    • A big bowl

    Each corn seed has a little water inside. In a hot pan it turns to steam and bursts the seed open: pop!

    Ask an adult: very hot pan

    What you need
    • Half a cup of popcorn seeds
    • 1 tablespoon oil
    • A big pan with a lid
    • A pinch of salt
