Questions

How do I get tickets?

Ask a grown-up to buy them at the door. Kids up to 12 pay half price.

Is it very loud?

Rock music is loud, and loud sound can hurt your ears. We give free ear plugs to everyone at the door.

Are there really pancakes?

Yes! After every show, Max makes pancakes for the first 50 fans.

Can I play with the band?

At every show, one fan comes up and plays the cowbell in "Big Stack Boogie".

Why ear plugs?

Deep inside your ear are tiny hair cells. They turn sound into signals for your brain. Very loud sound can harm them, and they do not grow back.
