A trip through time

  1. Triassic

    252 to 201 million years ago

    The first dinosaurs appear, about 230 million years ago. Many are small and run on two legs.

  2. Jurassic

    201 to 145 million years ago

    Giant plant-eaters like Brontosaurus and Stegosaurus. The first birds fly.

  3. Cretaceous

    145 to 66 million years ago

    T. rex, Triceratops and the first flowers. It ends when a huge asteroid hits Earth.

  4. Today

    66 million years ago to now

    The big dinosaurs are gone, but birds are still here. They are dinosaurs too!

Questions

Are the bones real?

Some are real fossils: bones that turned to stone. The biggest skeletons are copies, so the real bones stay safe.

Can I touch anything?

Yes! Look for the hand sign. At those tables you can touch fossil teeth and shells.

Did people live with dinosaurs?

No. The big dinosaurs died out 66 million years ago. The first people came millions of years later.

Is there a cafe?

Yes, right next to the T. rex hall. T. rex would have trouble holding the spoon.
