The area

Beaches, boats, forest paths and a lighthouse that blinks all night. Here is what to do.

Things to do

Summer or winter?

Long, light days

  • Swim. The water is shallow and warm near the hotel.
  • Rent a kayak. Paddle along the cliffs with a guide.
  • Catch crabs. Borrow a bucket and a line at the front desk.
  • Ice cream. The harbour kiosk has twenty flavours.

Short days, cosy nights

  • Storm watching. See the big waves from the warm lounge.
  • Sauna. Ours has a window that looks out over the sea.
  • Snow walks. We lend you boots for the forest path.
  • Hot chocolate. By the fire, with a good book.
