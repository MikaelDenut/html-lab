About this home

Robin Cottage is a white wooden house from 1912, with a red front door and a picket fence. Inside, everything was made new in 2024. The living room has a big window onto the garden, and the kitchen is bright and white.

Upstairs are three bedrooms and the bathroom. The smallest bedroom has an arched window that looks out over the trees. The beach is six minutes away on foot.
