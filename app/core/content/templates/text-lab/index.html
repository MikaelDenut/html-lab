Letters can do tricks

This page is a type specimen: a sheet that shows off what letters can do. Each box holds one trick, and the grey line under it names the CSS that makes it.

Put your mouse on a box, or press Tab to jump to it. Four of the boxes play.

Outline letters

Hollow

-webkit-text-stroke draws the edge, color: transparent empties the inside. Point at it to pour in red ink.

Writing downwards

TOWER

This line runs from the top down.

writing-mode: vertical-rl turns the line. text-orientation: upright stands each letter up. Japanese and Chinese can be written like this, in columns from right to left.

Letters in 3D

POP!

Eight text-shadow copies, each 1px further. Point at it to press it flat.

Letters that breathe

SPACE

letter-spacing puts air between the letters. Point at it.

A drop cap

Long ago, books were written by hand. The first letter of a chapter was often painted big and bright, in red or even in gold. When printing began, printers left an empty space, and a painter added that letter by hand.

::first-letter picks the first letter. float: left lets the lines flow round it.

Highlighter pen

A yellow pen marks the words that matter. This one even goes over two lines of text, and each line gets its own piece of yellow.

The yellow is a linear-gradient. Point at it: it grows from 0% to 100% wide.
