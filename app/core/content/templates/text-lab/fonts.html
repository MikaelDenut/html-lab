Fonts that go together

A font pair is one font for the titles and one for reading. A good pair is different but friendly: a fancy font with a plain one, a tall one with a round one.

Every font on this page is already on your Mac. To use a pair, copy its two font-family lines into style.css.

  • For a fashion magazine

    font-family: Didot, serif; font-family: "Avenir Next", sans-serif;

    Big Hats Are Back

    This one has a feather, a ribbon and a tiny bird's nest on top.

  • For a bike workshop

    font-family: Rockwell, serif; font-family: "Gill Sans", sans-serif;

    Fixed by Friday

    Bring your bike on Monday. We pump the tyres, oil the chain and tighten every bolt.

  • For a night train

    font-family: Futura, sans-serif; font-family: Baskerville, serif;

    Night Train North

    It leaves at nine. You sleep in a little bed that folds down from the wall.

  • For a circus poster

    font-family: Superclarendon, serif; font-family: Futura, sans-serif;

    Circus Tonight

    Jugglers, a tightrope and a dog that rides a bicycle. Bring a cushion!

  • For an invitation

    font-family: Copperplate, serif; font-family: "Hoefler Text", serif;

    You Are Invited

    Come to the castle garden for tea, cake and a very slow game of croquet.

  • For an ice cream van

    font-family: Phosphate, fantasy; font-family: "Chalkboard SE", sans-serif;

    Ice Cream Day!

    Three scoops, sprinkles on top and a wafer in the shape of a fish.

  • For a detective's notebook

    font-family: "American Typewriter", serif; font-family: Verdana, sans-serif;

    The Missing Sock

    At eight both socks were on the chair. At nine only the left one was there. Who took it?

  • For a sports page

    font-family: "DIN Condensed", sans-serif; font-family: Georgia, serif;

    Goal in the Last Minute

    The goalkeeper ran all the way up the field and scored with his head.

  • Twins that fight

    font-family: Didot, serif; font-family: "Bodoni 72", serif;

    Too Alike

    Both fonts are thin, tall and fancy, so your eye cannot tell the title from the text. Pick two fonts that are different!
