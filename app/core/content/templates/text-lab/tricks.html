Press, cut, underline

Four bigger tricks. Point at a box, or press Tab, and the pressed letters, the short story and the marble letters play.

Pressed into paper

Printed

A printing press pushes inked metal letters into the paper. On thick card it can leave a dent. Two text-shadows copy the dent: a dark edge 1px up and a light edge 1px down. Point at it to press harder.

Fancy underlines

wavy double thick

text-decoration-style makes the line wavy or double. text-decoration-thickness makes it fat, and text-underline-offset moves it down.

Text cut short

Once upon a time, a small metal letter A lived in a wooden box with all the other letters. Every morning the printer picked it up, put it in a line with its friends and pressed them onto paper. By the end of the day, the letter A had been in a hundred words: apple, cat, banana and many more.

-webkit-line-clamp: 3 stops the text after 3 lines and puts ... at the end. Shops and news sites use it for "read more" boxes. Point at it to read it all.

Letters cut from a picture

MARBLE

background-clip: text cuts the picture to the shape of the letters. The picture is marbled paper from France, about 1880: paint floats on water, and a sheet of paper picks it up. Point at it and the paper slides.
