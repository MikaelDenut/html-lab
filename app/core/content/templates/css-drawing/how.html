A house in six boxes

Here is the little house again, built one shape at a time. Each step adds one box. The new box is in colour, the old ones turn grey.

  1. 1. The wall A box with a background colour.

  2. 2. The roof clip-path cuts a box into a triangle.

  3. 3. The door border-radius rounds the top corners.

  4. 4. The window Two linear-gradient stripes make the cross.

  5. 5. The chimney It comes after the roof in the HTML, but z-index keeps the roof in front.

  6. 6. The smoke One puff and two box-shadow copies.

How to cut a triangle

clip-path: polygon() joins dots and cuts away the rest. Each dot is two numbers: how far across, then how far down the box.

The roof has class="house-roof tri". Its cut lives in the class tri, so every triangle can share it:

.tri {
  clip-path: polygon(50% 0, 100% 100%, 0 100%);
}

One house, three sizes

Every size in the house is in em. One em is as big as the font-size. So when the font-size grows, the whole house grows with it.
