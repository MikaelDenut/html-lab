Your doodle on a T-shirt

Every Wobblestitch pattern starts as a drawing by a kid. Stars, socks, sleepy dragons, it can be anything. The best doodles become next spring's clothes.

We make clothes for kids aged 4 to 12. They are soft, they stretch, and they are made for running, climbing and puddle jumping.
