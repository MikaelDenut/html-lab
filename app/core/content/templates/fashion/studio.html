From sketch to shop

How does a doodle turn into a T-shirt you can wear? Follow the six steps, from the first pencil line to the shop.

  1. 01

    Sketch

    Every T-shirt starts with a pencil. We draw lots of ideas, and kids send us their doodles too. Most ideas stay on paper. Only the best one moves on.

  2. 02

    Pick the colours

    We choose three or four colours that look good together. A colour wheel helps. Colours on opposite sides of the wheel make each other look brighter.

  3. 03

    Make a pattern

    A pattern is a small picture that repeats, like tiles on a wall. Look in style.css: one small circle, repeated, makes all the polka dots.

  4. 04

    Cut the fabric

    Paper shapes are laid on the fabric, and we cut around them. A T-shirt has a front, a back, two sleeves and a thin strip for the neck.

  5. 05

    Sew it together

    A sewing machine joins the pieces. T-shirt fabric stretches, so the stitches must stretch too. A zig-zag stitch can stretch without breaking.

  6. 06

    Off to the shop

    Last, every T-shirt is checked and folded. A small label inside tells the size and how to wash it. Then it is ready for you!
