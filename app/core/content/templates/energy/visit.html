Safety first

  • Wear the hard hat we give you.
  • Stay on the paths with your guide.
  • Never climb a fence or a gate.
  • In winter, ice can slide off the blades. We keep a big safe circle around each turbine.

Questions people ask

How big is a turbine?

Our towers are 100 metres tall, and each blade is 60 metres long. Stand at the bottom and look up: it is like a building with 30 floors.

Can I go inside a turbine?

Only our engineers climb up. Inside each tower there is a long ladder and a small lift. In the visitor centre you can sit in a real turbine head.

How fast do the blades turn?

They turn slowly, about 10 to 15 times a minute. But the tips go round such a big circle that they move at more than 200 km an hour.

What happens when there is no wind?

The turbines rest. The solar panels can still work, and power from other places flows in through the wires.

Is it noisy?

Next to a turbine you hear a soft whoosh, whoosh, whoosh. Bring your ears and listen!
