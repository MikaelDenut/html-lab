What our turbines do in every wind

  1. 0 m/s

    Calm. The blades rest.

  2. 3 m/s

    Light breeze. The blades start to turn.

  3. 12 m/s

    Strong breeze. Now each turbine makes its full power.

  4. 25 m/s

    Storm, 90 km an hour. The turbines stop to stay safe.

m/s means metres every second. Our 24 turbines turn to face the wind all by themselves.

What we do

Our wind farm

Some wind farms, like this one in a desert, have hundreds of turbines. Ours, up on Gustwell Hill, has 24. The wind blows there nearly every day, so the turbines turn to face it and their long blades spin.

When a storm comes, they stop and wait. Safety first!

From wind to your plug

Solar fields by the river

Solar panels turn sunlight into electricity, with no moving parts at all. They even work on cloudy days, but they make less.

Sheep keep the grass short between the rows.
