Meet the crew

Six pirates run every treasure hunt. Five of them have two legs. One of them has wings.

Captain Marigold Rumbletum

The captain

Marigold plans every hunt and hides the treasure chest. She writes all the riddles herself, and she laughs so loud that the gulls fly away.

"A good pirate shares. A great pirate shares the last biscuit."

Ossi Knotwell

The navigator

Ossi reads the map and the compass. Lost? Ask Ossi. He always knows where north is.

Pip Spyglass

The lookout

Pip climbs up high and spots the next clue first. Pip has the sharpest eyes in the club.

Tilly Tidewell

The bosun

On a ship, the bosun looks after the ropes and the sails. Tilly looks after the crews and the first-aid box.

Bram Butterbun

The cook

Bram makes the pirate snacks: apple boats and cheese wheels. He also guards the chocolate coins.

Noodle

The ship's parrot

Noodle can say "Ahoy!" and "Biscuit!". She is made of green felt, so please do not feed her crackers.

Pirate words

Sailors have their own words for the parts of a ship. Learn them and talk like a real crew.

Ahoy!
Hello! Sailors shout it to another ship.
Aye
Yes.
Bow
The front of a ship.
Stern
The back of a ship.
Port
The left side of a ship, when you look to the front. Port and left both have four letters!
Starboard
The right side of a ship, when you look to the front.
Galley
The kitchen on a ship.
Crow's nest
A lookout place high up on the mast.
Doubloon
An old Spanish gold coin.
Landlubber
Someone who is new to the sea.
Shipshape
Neat and tidy.
Scallywag
A cheeky rascal.
