The Great Treasure Hunt

Once a month, the crews follow the clues through Gullwhistle Park. The hunt takes about two hours, and every pirate goes home with treasure.

The rules, clue by clue

  1. Find your crew

    A crew is 3 or 4 pirates and one grown-up. Choose a crew name together.

  2. Get your map piece

    Every crew gets one piece of the map and a compass at Biscuit Beach. That is the sandpit by the park gate.

  3. Solve the riddle

    Each map piece has a riddle on the back. It tells you where the next clue is hidden.

  4. Walk, do not run

    Stay on the paths, and stay where your grown-up can see you.

  5. Collect five stamps

    Every clue box has a stamp. Stamp your crew card, then put the clue back for the next crew.

  6. Find the X

    With five stamps, the last riddle shows you the way to the big red X.

  7. Share the treasure

    Open the chest together. Every pirate gets the same share. That is our pirate code!

What is in the chest?
