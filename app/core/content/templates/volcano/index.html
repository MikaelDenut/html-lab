How an eruption happens

  1. Deep under the ground, rock gets so hot that it melts. Melted rock is called magma.
  2. Magma is lighter than the rock around it, so it rises and fills the magma chamber.
  3. Gas bubbles in the magma push hard, and the magma climbs up the vent.
  4. It bursts out of the crater as lava, ash and gas.
